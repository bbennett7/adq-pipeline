Example questions and answers:
1. Q: "What is an agent **_harness_**?"
   A: "An agent harness is the infrastructure wrapped around an AI model that turns it from a basic text generator into a functional agent. It includes things like prompts, tool definitions, instructions and control loops, which work together to define an agent's context and guardrails."
2. Q: "When does an AI become an **_agent_**?"
   A: "An AI becomes an agent when it can take **actions in the world on its own**, not just produce text for a human to act on. Agents have **tools** to work with, can iterate in a **loop** to produce stronger results, and make **decisions**."
3. Q: "How is a **_GPU_** different than a **_CPU_**?"
   A: "CPUs (Central Processing Units) and GPUs (Graphics Processing Units) are both processors, which are the chips that do the actual computing work in a device. /n A **CPU is the main brain of your computer**. It runs your operating system, your browser, your apps — basically everything. It has a handful of powerful cores (usually 4 to 16) that can each handle complicated instructions and switch between tasks quickly. /n In contrast, **GPUs were built with thousands of small, simple cores that all work in parallel**. They were designed to be the chips that drew images on your screen (think rendering games or video), but this same functionality made them optimal for training and running neural networks. Because of their differing structures, **a task that would take a CPU hours can take a GPU minutes**."
